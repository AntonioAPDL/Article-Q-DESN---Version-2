# PriceFM Stage-R111B: BG exposure-aligned readout

## Decision

Stage-R111A is complete and frozen. It resolved the EE neighbor-driver mechanism, but it did not authorize a broad all-region campaign. The remaining bounded mechanism question is BG: its frozen Q-DESN readout was fitted on recent observed-price exposure, whereas outer validation applies that readout to states induced by recursively forecast prices. Stage-R111B tests whether aligning the readout's training exposure with deployment improves BG without changing the reservoir, likelihood family, prior, or validation driver.

This is the smallest scientifically informative next experiment. It is one region, three folds, one frozen AL family, one frozen DESN, one frozen Q-DESN `tau0`, and one frozen Normal-RHS driver policy. It does not reopen the broad DESN search, AL/exAL selection, Normal-driver selection, test set, registry, article, joint model, or MCMC.

## Diagnosis

The BG failure is not explained by a missing target-price driver: R110 supplied that driver and improved over the first frozen replay, but remained worse than the direct R97 reference. The strongest remaining mismatch is the readout's covariate exposure. Its coefficients were learned from reservoir states generated with recent observed prices, then evaluated on states generated by recursive forecast prices. Re-screening reservoir size, `tau0`, or likelihood family would confound this mechanism question and repeat already completed work.

R111B therefore changes only the readout training design. The target outcome and all fixed priors remain unchanged.

## Exposure construction

For outer fold `f`, the existing training origins are partitioned into three embargoed temporal cross-fit blocks. For each block `k`, the frozen R110 Normal-RHS policy is fitted using only observations before that block. It generates 500 recursive target-price paths for the held-out origins.

For origin `i`, horizon `h`, and path `s`, let `phi(i,h,s)` be the frozen BG reservoir/readout feature vector produced by recursively feeding path `s` into the target-price lag. The recursive training feature is

```text
X_recursive(i,h) = (1 / 500) sum_s phi(i,h,s).
```

The bounded arms are:

| Arm | Training rows | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `teacher_recent` | observed-price exposure only | exact control |
| `recursive_mean` | mean recursive exposure only | direct deployment alignment |
| `mixed_equal` | equal stacked observed and recursive rows | robust compromise |

The mean recursive state is a deliberate, bounded errors-in-variables approximation. Raw paths are retained in the driver artifacts, so a later pathwise objective can be designed if this experiment shows promise. No Cartesian `500 x 500` expansion is used.

## Selection and fitting

1. Fit 9 cross-fit Normal-RHS drivers: 3 outer folds by 3 inner temporal blocks.
2. Build 9 paired teacher/recursive exposure blocks.
3. In outer fold 1 training only, fit the median AL-RHS readout for 3 arms by 3 leave-one-block-out splits.
4. Evaluate every held-out split on recursive exposure, regardless of the arm used for fitting.
5. Select by mean held-out scaled AQL, then worst-block AQL, then deterministic priority `recursive_mean`, `mixed_equal`, `teacher_recent`.
6. Freeze that one arm for all three outer folds.
7. Fit seven AL-RHS quantiles on all three cross-fit blocks in each outer fold: 21 final fits.
8. Replay the existing 500 R110 BG outer-validation driver paths and close out all three folds.

Median-screen fits start from zero coefficients and a scale estimated only from their training blocks. This prevents a full-training R103 initializer from carrying held-out-block information into arm selection. Final fits may use the matching R103 AL posterior as a computational warm start because it was fitted only on the same outer-training window. In every case the destination prior stays zero-centered RHS-NS with fixed `tau0 = 1e-4`; the initializer never defines a prior center.

## Work and scheduler contract

| Phase | Tasks | Model fits |
|---|---:|---:|
| Cross-fit Normal drivers | 9 | 9 |
| Exposure builders | 9 | 0 |
| Median arm screen | 9 | 9 |
| Final seven-quantile fits | 21 | 21 |
| Outer-validation replay | 3 | 0 |
| **Total** | **51** | **39** |

The requested worker count is a ceiling of 30. The launcher detects currently idle physical cores, reserves one free physical core, and binds each active task to one distinct physical core. Every child receives single-thread BLAS/OpenMP settings. The largest fitting wave has 21 models, so the launcher will not manufacture redundant work merely to occupy 30 cores.

Each phase is dependency ordered and restartable. A task is reused only when its terminal status, task-contract hash, artifact existence, and artifact hashes validate. The frozen campaign contract also requires the launch commit and every source/evidence hash to match.

## Closeout gate

R111B passes only if all conditions hold:

1. all 3 BG folds complete with exactly 500 paths;
2. pooled AQL improves at least 10% over the R110 frozen-Q-DESN replay;
3. no fold is harmed by more than 5% versus that replay;
4. pooled AQL is no more than 10% above the R97 direct reference;
5. raw quantile crossing is at most 1%;
6. all reported metrics are finite.

A pass resolves the BG mechanism and permits design of Stage-R112. It does not authorize an all-region launch, registry mutation, article update, MCMC, or joint fitting. A failure stops this recursive-readout branch and retains R97 as the scientific reference.
