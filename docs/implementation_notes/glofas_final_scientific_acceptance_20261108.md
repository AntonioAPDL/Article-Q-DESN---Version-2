# GloFAS final scientific acceptance gate

## Purpose

This gate closes the scientific review that must precede any merge of the
corrected GloFAS article projection. It does not fit, continue, select or
retune a model. It verifies one frozen composition:

- Search III remains the authority for Parts 1--3;
- the completed October unit-member campaign is the qualified Part 4 target;
- the November same-family campaign remains adverse diagnostic evidence only.

The gate is deliberately stricter than a successful runtime. It joins target
identity, recursive-driver contracts, numerical qualification, future-truth
isolation, path ordering, provenance and exact article-payload validation.

## Scientific decision

The catastrophic November crossings were reproduced by a global complete-path
reference. They were not caused by the local Gaussian--Delta approximation,
Monte Carlo error or a short optimizer run. The rejected target used seven
separate level-specific future-response transition laws and therefore did not
define one common predictive response distribution.

The corrected target uses one shared, truth-free Normal-RHS recursive path law.
Parts 1--3 feed 500 aligned paths through each quantile readout. Part 4 uses
the corresponding joint Gaussian path prior and all 51 issued GloFAS member
factors. The future-USGS AL/exAL working likelihood is replaced, not
multiplied, and held-out USGS is read only after the packet is sealed for
scoring.

No additional fit is scientifically justified for this defect because the
complete October campaign already implements that coherent target and passes
its fit-side gates. Rerunning it would duplicate the same posterior target.

## Acceptance interpretation

Acceptance does not require exAL to outperform AL. A weaker score is a valid
empirical result when the target, lineage and numerical gates pass. It also
does not convert the unavailable post-cutoff retrospective GloFAS series into
data: Part 2 remains explicitly not evaluable on that protected window.

The discrepancy concern is handled in two pieces. Search III's reference and
discrepancy geometries remain frozen from internal rainy-season development
and confirmation; the protected window did not choose them. The Part 2 final
forecast is retained but unscored because its defining future retrospective
quantity is unavailable. This is a documented data limitation, not a software
pass or a fabricated performance claim.

## Hard gates

1. Root-cause manifest and structural-incoherence decision reproduce.
2. The coherent-driver audit reports 3/3 banks, 48/48 Part 1--3 contracts,
   48/48 covariance-bearing quantile fits, 28/28 Part 4 cores, zero truth reads
   and zero fits.
3. The source-bound Part 4 qualification passes 18/18 fit and quadrature gates.
4. All 16 Part 4 quantile cores use the same Normal-driver prior hash, no
   future-USGS likelihood and all 1,428 issued-member factors.
5. Search III Parts 1 and 3 have complete frozen scoring surfaces; Part 2 is
   uniformly and explicitly marked not evaluable.
6. Corrected Part 4 quantile crossings are at most one; no isotonic repair is
   used as the scientific explanation.
7. Protected-window scores are descriptive only and make zero method choices.
8. The article payload commit passes its committed authority guard and contains
   no active November alias.
9. Every input and output is sealed by SHA256.

## Outputs

`455_build_glofas_final_scientific_acceptance.R` writes a self-contained,
ignored packet containing final gates, a component-by-family status table, an
issue-closure ledger, frozen Parts 1--4 metrics, evidence hashes, a compact PDF
atlas, a machine-readable receipt and an artifact manifest.

`456_check_glofas_final_scientific_acceptance.R` verifies the sealed packet
without consulting mutable runtime directories. The focused test mutates
target, evaluation and crossing assumptions to confirm that the gate fails
closed.

`GLOFAS_SCIENTIFIC_ACCEPTANCE_PASS_READY_FOR_CONTROLLED_PROMOTION` may be bound
to the article candidate. That binding authorizes coordinator review and a
controlled merge; it does not itself merge main, update Overleaf or delete
runtime evidence.
