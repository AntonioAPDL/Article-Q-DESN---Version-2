seconds. It priced the full worst-case B1 allocation at29,968.984634CPU seconds
against21,786.141935CPU seconds of conservative projected idle service from
the original finite three-wave computing ceiling. The original controller
correctly stopped before forecasting under that source's full-worst-branch
admission predicate.

Requiring the conditional maximum before admitting any useful work was an
unnecessary restriction on the agreed finite-batch plan. The whole72-new-D
surface costs7,196.446389 projected CPU seconds. The entire mandatory prefix,
including the worst prospectively possible A and its already frozen SHA audit

21,786.141935CPU service budget. No new score was inspected to make this choice.

The successor therefore fixes B1 and admits this entire scientific prefix.
It neither truncates the deterministic pool nor claims a partially evaluated
pool is complete. It imports the existing benchmark instead of repeating it,
and retains exactly the same three two-hour numerical wave opportunities,
90minute dispatch budgets, reserve time, idle priority and physical CPUs15–29.

observed idle-share adjustment. There is no extra benchmark, renewed lease,

## The conditional MC budget is atomic

After the whole78-gross-D surface verifies, A remains the strongest noncontrol
under the primary equal-three-split macro, with ties1e-10EUR/MWh resolved by
stable configuration ID. B remains the fixed SHA permutation's first geometry
distinct from C0/C5/A. No cost-based nominee replacement is permitted.

The same full-cohort original-seed S checks and half-practical-margin trigger
determine the qualifier set. Before any extra seed dispatch, freeze the sum of:

1. all admitted deterministic components;
2. actual nominated seed0 components and the matched original C0 comparator;
3. both full extra seeds2026102301/2026102302 for every qualifier and shared C0.

For completed prefix work, a prospectively frozen accounting rule releases
unused conservative reservation only with immutable observed-CPU evidence.
Each owned InvocationID records task/source/policy identity at startup. After
strict scientific completion, an independently hashed CPU receipt binds the
final prediction seal, whole-process CPU and CPU of terminated child processes.
Its observation point precedes receipt writing and exit, so add a5CPU-second
allowance per observed attempt. This is neither an exact exit measurement nor
a hard private CPU quota. New, unexecuted work retains its full frozen1.5×
forecast cost.

All verified checkpoint attempts are summed, each with its own tail allowance.
An unknown/malformed or abruptly interrupted attempt retains a full frozen
component reservation; a resume with preexisting checkpoints but no previous
owned CPU provenance cannot release that reservation. Observed overruns raise
the charge instead of being clipped. Missing CPU receipts do not invalidate a
scientifically sealed result, but cannot create compute credit. The atomic
accounting also checks the union of recorded earlier InvocationIDs: a deleted
referenced attempt retains an unknown-work reservation. At least one validated
completed CPU receipt must bind the current final prediction seal; surviving
partial-only observations retain an additional full final-work reservation.
The atomic
decision records every completed-component charge and its provenance. Workers
must have drained before this phase decision, avoiding the small interval
between a visible scientific seal and its CPU receipt.

If completed observed CPU plus tail allowances/fallback reservations and the
conservative **future** extra bundle fit the original available service, the
whole qualifier-plus-control extra bundle is admitted. Otherwise
**all** of that required extra bundle remains explicitly unfunded/pending. The
runner does not discard a costly qualifier, replace it, alter seeds, weaken a
gain threshold, cherry-pick a seed or secretly consume another wave. An empty
ready queue in this state is a resource deferral, never scientific completion.
The completed D and seed0 results remain usable development evidence, with
matched-three-seed sensitivity explicitly unresolved.

If no nominee triggers extra seeds, the stage can close as a negative bounded
screen after direct retained-array validation. If funded extras all complete,
the original practical and MC stability gates determine at most one separately
budgeted AL proposal. No AL work is launched. Retained-output, uncertainty,
operator,500-path AL, cached-C0/21-new-fit and independent-confirmation limits
remain as documented in the original plan and scientific implementation note.

is rejected before imports or admission. `finalize` independently recomputes

unfunded or incomplete MC set. Baseline, AT, authority, main and article remain
