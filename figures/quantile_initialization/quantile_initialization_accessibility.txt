Sequential initialization for a quantile grid containing the median. A preliminary Gaussian ridge regression supplies starting values for Gaussian regularized-horseshoe variational Bayes, which initializes the median asymmetric-Laplace regression. Independent AL fits proceed outward toward each tail. Each completed AL fit initializes exAL at its own probability level. Complete independent grids initialize matching joint variational fits only in the joint simulation and GloFAS application; PriceFM uses independent AL/exAL variational fits only. The lower-left relation shows VB initializing MCMC for the same statistical model in the joint simulation. GloFAS and PriceFM use variational inference. The independent single-quantile validation follows a separate procedure. Solid arrows indicate transfers of starting values, not prior calibration or specification selection. The dashed lower-right arrow denotes comparison of fitted candidate specifications using a prespecified development criterion. Any Gaussian-derived prior calibration is separate from these transfers and fixed before the subsequent comparison.
