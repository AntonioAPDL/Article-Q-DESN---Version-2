# PriceFM Stage R143: CZ Quarantine and Queue Recovery

## Decision

R143 changes orchestration only. The frozen R141 scientific protocol remains
the source of every fit. CZ is quarantined after the bounded R142 rescue failed
four split-1/split-2 median AL fits. The remaining Jerez slot-B regions continue
through the exact committed R141 entrypoint on physical cores 15--29.

No CZ artifact is replaced or deleted. No certificate limit, posterior target,
model specification, training row, validation rule, test-data rule, registry,
article file, or authority is changed.

## Root diagnosis

The failed R142 attempt-2 certificates separate two questions:

- The linear systems are solved accurately: relative residuals are about
  `3e-14` to `4e-14`.
- The saved variational means remain `1.51e-4` to `1.71e-4` posterior standard
  deviations from the next coefficient update, above the frozen `1e-4` limit.

The precision matrices are ill-conditioned (`9.8e13` to `3.75e14`), but the
accurate solve residuals show that a failed direct solve is not the immediate
cause. The fits are finite and nearly stationary, yet they do not satisfy the
predeclared certificate after 900 cumulative attempted iterations. More blind
warm-start attempts or relaxed thresholds are therefore rejected.

The next admissible CZ investigation is training-only: evaluate the next
ranked already-screened RHS/DESN finalist under a separately frozen fallback
contract. It must not use final-test AQL and must not be mixed with R141 until
its complete provenance and certification are sealed.

## Queue contract

1. Verify Jerez, the exact clean R141 branch and HEAD, source file hashes, the
   source-matched release receipt, physical-core uniqueness, and host reserves.
2. Verify that the original scheduler records CZ as the failed slot-B region,
   that no slot-B R141 controller is alive, and that no R142 rescue is alive.
3. Record a read-only CZ diagnosis from all attempt-2 certificates.
4. Run `ES`, `HR`, `IT_SARD`, `LV`, `NO_3`, `PT`, and `SE_3` sequentially via
   the exact R141 prepare/controller entrypoints.
5. If one region fails, quarantine it and continue with the rest of the queue.
   A failed region never authorizes a scientific fallback automatically.
6. Preserve resumability by recognizing certified R141 closeouts and adopting
   a live controller after a supervisor restart.

R143 is non-promotional. Final comparison and authority decisions remain
blocked until all 38 regions and 114 folds are reconciled.
