## Historical fitted curves

The pure GloFAS helper takes already authenticated in-memory design and fit
objects. Historical USGS rows have zero discrepancy columns. For fixed-level
AL/exAL fits, the reported functional is the VB mean of the historical
conditional quantile, obtained from the stored reference design and
coefficient mean. For Normal fits, the corresponding mean conditional
quantile uses the exact inverse-gamma variance half-moment rather than the
square root of the mean variance.

The labels distinguish transformed-scale mean conditional quantiles from
future marginal response quantiles and from original-scale posterior means.
Raw fitted crossings are retained. No historical seven-quantile raw-GloFAS
fan is invented from a single retrospective value. The helper neither reads
files nor invokes saved builders; actual model/design extraction still needs
the complete accepted cohort and its own finite, authenticated read purpose.

`glofas_fitted_conditional_quantile_history_20261102.md`. Its dedicated
