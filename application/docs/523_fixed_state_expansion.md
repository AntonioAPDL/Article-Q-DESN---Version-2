original-recipe scalar reproduction, allowing no assertion of absent bitwise
original vectors or exact optimizer resume. A cross-host mismatch is evidence
to investigate runtime/source-equivalence before drawing an arithmetic cause.

explain a failed gate and is supported by unchanged-target equivalence. A small
